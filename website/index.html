THE INSTRUMENT

Made for the take.
Not the tap.

CamX starts where phone-camera automation ends: with an operator choosing the exposure, cadence, focus, and image profile before the record button turns red.

It reads what the camera hardware actually exposes. No invented lenses. No fictional formats. No frame rate the device cannot hold.

The result is quieter than a camera app and more immediate than a camera menu: a viewfinder with a point of view.
