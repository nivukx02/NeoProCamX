How should the repeating mode rail feel?

Swipe horizontally. The label aligned over the shutter becomes active. The order loops continuously: VIDEO - PRO - NDR - LOG - RAW - VIDEO.

RAWVIDEO PRO NDRLOG

A. Five-label cinema rail

Recommended. The user can see the sequence and the next choices while the centered active label stays aligned to the shutter.

VIDEO PRO NDR

B. Focused three-label rail

Larger spacing and less clutter, but the cyclic context is less visible because only adjacent modes appear.

RAW VIDEO PRO NDR LOG

C. Faded-edge cinema rail

Shows five cyclic modes with quieter far neighbors. More refined visually, but has reduced peripheral readability.

Resolution menu rule

The recording resolution selector will contain only 1K, 2K, 4K, and 8K; unsupported tiers are omitted rather than replaced by device-specific extra dimensions.
