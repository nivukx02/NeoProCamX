Adjusted From Your Note

  • Controller height reduced from a large drawer to a compact 132dp strip target.
  • Scale bar moves beside the Auto/Manual badge instead of consuming the whole top area.
  • Mode rail and shutter dock are tightened so the capture area feels more like a stock Android camera.
  • A small live status capsule above the controller keeps important values visible without another large panel.
  • In implementation, this strip appears only in Pro mode and can collapse when the user taps the preview.
